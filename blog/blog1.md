## What is Data Science to Me?

To me, Data Science is the overarching study of reasoning. For all decisions is a reason behind them, and Data Science is the study of such. Anybody is able to gather information and come to a conclusion, but Data Scientists are responsible for gathering and analyzing mass amounts of data that require tools that take time to master.

## The Importance of Modeling Data

Modeling social topics and people is a key part of Data Science, and is extremely important. It can be gathered from a business perspective, like trying to optimize workflow or increase customer spending. It also can be gathered from a social perspective, finding out why people prefer one candidate over the other, or what imporvements would increase human development.

## The Responsibilities of Data Analysis

However, there are a plethora of responsibilites that come with analyzing data. Data can be easily manipulated, and come from anyone capable. False correlations are a common issue that stem from this. For example: although both ice cream sales and murder rates rise in the summer season, this is not due to ice cream sales, and the Data Analysis does not need to give the context that being outdoors, better weather, and more social gatherings are more of the reason that the murder rates rise during summer. Intentionally leaving out those details and only referencing one variable like ice cream sales, forces closed minded people to carry the conclusion as a weapon. That is extremely harmful for society and human development.
